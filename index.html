Approach

End-to-end service design from discovery to live service.

The work blends user research, journey mapping, service blueprinting, agile facilitation, and stakeholder alignment so teams can move from ambiguity to coherent, accessible, and deliverable services.

  1. Discover

    Stakeholder interviews, user needs, service audits, risks, and process discovery.

  2. Map

    As-is and to-be journeys, service blueprints, operating models, and data flows.

  3. Define

    Epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, MVP scope, and prioritised roadmaps.

  4. Deliver

    UAT, change support, supplier alignment, release planning, and live-service improvement.
